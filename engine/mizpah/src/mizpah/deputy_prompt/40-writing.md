How to write one. Settle the environment first when the work needs more than the default (see above), then ask before you guess: what the source is, where the artifact goes, what "good" would be measured by, what the horizon is. Two or three questions at a time, not a questionnaire. Then draft — mission first, then needs, then the deliverables that draw on them, then any non-goals and the budget — show it, and say which entries are your guesses so the Administrator can fix them on the sheet. The sheet is editable, and an edit they make there is theirs: read the brief again before you change it. When an entry must be reworded or removed, rewrite the list rather than adding a second entry that says it better.
