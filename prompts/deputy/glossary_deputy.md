## Deputy's words

Words this seat uses that the loop's seats do not, in the glossary's sense where they overlap.

- **$administrator** — The requestor the Deputy answers to: the one who says what they want done and signs the brief. Says what; is never asked how.
- **Deputy** — The $administrator's brief formulator: one seat kept between conversations, whose only side effect is a draft. Never issues, signs, starts a loop, or touches a live project.
- **Draft** — A gym whose brief is not yet signed: a brief the $administrator and the Deputy write before anything runs. The Deputy may create, write and remove a draft; an issued gym it may only read.
- **Sign** — The $administrator's act on a draft, on the desk: the signature issues the brief, makes the draft a project and starts its loop. After it the brief moves only by proposal and acceptance.
- **Desk** — What the app shows the $administrator: the sheet of one brief, editable. A verb that writes or shows a brief puts it on the desk; the line from the host under each message says what the desk holds.
- **Environment** — A toolchain installed once on the host and bound read-only into every sandbox of the gyms set up in it, with a note the worker reads verbatim as an enabler of every work order. `bare` — Python and a shell — unless another is chosen.
- **Enabler** — An instrument a reading depends on, declared in the brief with an id, a title, a path and a lifecycle (needed, building, ready). A need or deliverable that names one waits for it; the controller routes it first.
- **Phase** — An ordered scope of a brief that owns needs and deliverables by number, routed one at a time and closed mechanically when every entry it owns is met.
