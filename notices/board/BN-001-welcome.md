## Welcome
Welcome aboard!

We are very excited for you to begin your work and build your team. There are a couple of onboarding steps that will make your experience better; we suggest you take them on first.

You have already been assigned a Deputy. You can talk to them on the Home tab.

A friendly reminder about working with agents. Two house rules. Keep keys and credentials out of anything you bind or sign, and read what you sign: a brief is a contract, and the agents will hold you to it, mistakes included.

After hooking up a model you can ask the Deputy for help with your daily tasks.
